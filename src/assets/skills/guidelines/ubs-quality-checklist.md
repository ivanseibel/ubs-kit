# UBS Quality Checklist

## Structure

- [ ] All 11 canonical sections are present and in order
- [ ] Section headings match canonical names exactly
- [ ] Identity names exactly one primary behavior

## Clarity and Completeness

- [ ] Business Context explains the intent clearly
- [ ] Actors are explicit and complete
- [ ] Initial State and Triggering Event are unambiguous
- [ ] Rules and Guards are deterministic
- [ ] Expected Outcome is observable

## Safety and Assumptions

- [ ] Invalid States or Outcomes are listed
- [ ] Invariants are explicit
- [ ] Assumptions have unique IDs and TODO questions

## Observability

- [ ] Minimum Observability lists signals required to verify behavior
